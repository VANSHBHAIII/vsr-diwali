Why do we celebrate Diwali?

Let's understand it in a simple and fun way.

🌟

Good over evil

Diwali reminds us that goodness, truth and kindness can overcome darkness and negativity.

🏹

Lord Rama's return

In a well-known tradition, people celebrate Lord Rama, Sita and Lakshmana returning to Ayodhya after 14 years.

🪔

Light and hope

People light diyas to welcome happiness, hope and positivity into their homes and lives.

🤝

Togetherness

Families and friends meet, share sweets, decorate homes and spread love and good wishes.
